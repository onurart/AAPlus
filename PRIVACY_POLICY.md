# Privacy Policy for AAPlus

**Last Updated:** May 5, 2025

## Overview
AAPlus ("the App") is a free mobile game developed as a personal project. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information related to your use of the App.

## Information We Collect
**We do not collect any personal data.** The App does not:
- Require account creation or login
- Collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers
- Track your location
- Access your contacts, camera, or microphone
- Use analytics or tracking services
- Share any data with third parties

## Data Stored Locally
The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**:
- High score
- Current level progress
- Sound and haptic feedback preferences

This data is stored using the device's local storage (SharedPreferences on Android) and is **never transmitted** to any server.

## Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate any third-party services such as:
- Advertising SDKs
- Analytics platforms
- Social media SDKs
- Crash reporting tools

## Children's Privacy
The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is suitable for all ages.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
